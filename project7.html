Commercial Building

📑 Project Description

Project Overview

This is a design project for a commercial building consisting of a ground floor, five typical floors, and a roof. The lighting simulation and layout were fully completed using DIALux Evo, in compliance with the Egyptian Code.

My Role

My personal contribution to the team project was handling the entire project design process. This included ensuring proper lighting distribution, power system and load management, fire alarm system, light current, cable tray, scheduals , sld and rizers.

Lighting Design Stage

The design covers all floors and includes both normal and emergency lighting fixtures. Fixtures were carefully selected based on functionality and interior aesthetics, and include:

  • 60x60 LED panels
  • Circular spotlights
  • LED profiles
  • Pendant fixtures
  • Surface-mounted lights
  • Decorative chandeliers

Simulation results were optimized for lux levels and energy efficiency, forming a solid foundation for future load calculation and layout detailing.

System Distribution

Systmes are distributed and documented on Revit. Each system had a separate lighting and power panel and circuits were connected appropriately. schedules were created, and circuit protection was applied based on Egyptian standards.

Softwares Used

  • DIALux Evo – For lighting simulation
  • Revit – For Design and Layouts
